How It Works — Terrain Models & Cost Functions

The Martian Route & EVA Planner models human metabolic expenditure across planetary terrain using calibrated bioenergetic equations and high-resolution NASA laser altimetry data.

Minetti Bioenergetics Equation (Mars Calibrated):
C_w(i) = 280.5·i⁵ - 58.7·i⁴ - 289.8·i³ + 74.7·i² + 19.1·i + 3.28 [J/(kg·m)]

Where i = tan(θ) is the terrain gradient slope and gravity scaling factor is set to 0.378g.
