Beginner Guide & Operational Manual

WHAT IS THIS SYSTEM?

The Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Route & EVA Planner is a hybrid geospatial-graph-AI application designed for planning safe surface Extravehicular Activities (EVAs) for astronauts exploring **Jezero Crater, Mars**.

WHY DO WE NEED IT? (THE MARS SURVIVAL PROBLEM)

Mars surface terrain presents extreme hazards: steep crater drop-offs ($>20^\circ$), loose regolith scree, sharp basalt boulders, and zero atmospheric pressure. Astronaut xEMU spacesuits have strict life support constraints (90-120 minutes of $O_2$ and battery). Walking in a straight line over a crater rim can cause slips, suit tears, or metabolic overexertion.

Cost Function: C = Δd + (k · e^θ)

Our engine uses an exponential penalty function $C = \Delta d + k \cdot e^\theta$ to penalize slope steepness ($\theta$), routing astronauts *around* hazardous cliffs rather than over them.

HOW TO USE THE SYSTEM (STEP-BY-STEP TUTORIAL)

  1. Select Mission Route: Use preset locations (e.g. *Perseverance Landing Site ➔ Neretva Delta*) or click "Click Map Pins" to set custom pins anywhere on the satellite map.
  2. Choose Strategy: Select 🛡️ SAFEST (avoids all slopes), ⚖️ BALANCED (optimal balance), or ⚡ FASTEST (direct travel).
  3. Set Incline Ceiling: Adjust the Max Slope Barrier slider (default $15^\circ$) to enforce your suit's safe slope threshold.
  4. Compute Route: Click 04 COMPUTE EVA ROUTE to trigger the A* algorithm and AI briefing generation.
  5. Inspect Telemetry & Briefing: Analyze the red path overlay on the map, hover on the elevation profile chart, check suit consumable metrics, and review the NASA Flight Director Safety Briefing.
