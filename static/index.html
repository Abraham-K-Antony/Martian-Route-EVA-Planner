How the Martian EVA Planner Works

Walking on Mars is fundamentally different from hiking on Earth. Explore the physical bioenergetics, IAU planetary coordinate systems, and NASA life-support limits powering our traversal engine.

The Martian EVA Survival Problem

Astronauts exploring Jezero Crater wear NASA Exploration Extravehicular Mobility Units (xEMU), weighing approximately 140 kg (plus 80 kg astronaut mass = 220 kg total). While Mars gravity ($0.378g$) reduces weight to ~83 kg-force, inertia remains identical to Earth. Moving across loose regolith, steep crater scarps ($>20^\circ$), and sharp impact ejecta requires massive energy expenditure.

COMBINED MASS 220 kg 80kg astronaut + 140kg suit
SURFACE GRAVITY 3.71 m/s² 0.378 × Earth gravity
SUIT RIGIDITY FACTOR 1.35× Pressurized joint resistance

Governing Equations & Physics Models

1. Minetti Metabolic Energy Cost Function ($J/\text{m}$)

Adapted from Minetti et al. (2002) for terrestrial gradient walking, scaled for reduced gravity and pressurized spacesuit mobility restrictions:

C(i) = M_total · (280.5i⁵ - 58.7i⁴ - 76.8i³ + 26.8i² + 19.6i + 2.5) · (g_mars/g_earth) · 1.35

where $i = \tan(\theta)$ is slope grade, with an empirical floor of 150 J/m.

2. Slope-Dependent Traversal Speed ($m/s$)

Walking speed decreases sharply on ascending grades and loose scree according to Tobler's hiking law with Mars safety bounds:

v(i) = v_flat · exp(-3.5 · |i + 0.05|)

Bounded by a minimum safe traversal speed floor of $0.15\text{ m/s}$ ($0.54\text{ km/h}$).

3. IAU Mars Radius DEM Resolution ($m/\text{px}$)

Earth WGS84 ellipsoids do not apply to Mars. We compute metric grid distance using the International Astronomical Union (IAU) Mars volumetric mean radius:

dy = Δlat · (π/180) · 3,389,500 m, dx = Δlon · (π/180) · 3,389,500 m · cos(lat_center)

yielding an exact spatial DEM resolution of $38.4\text{ m/px}$ across Jezero Crater.

Assumptions & Educational Simulation Scope

This application is designed as an educational and mission-architecture planning simulation for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. While built on real Mars GeoTIFF DEMs and peer-reviewed biomechanical formulas, it is not certified for flight operations or real human spaceflight EVAs. Actual Mars missions require sub-meter LIDAR, active rover scouting, and real-time PLSS sensor telemetry.

System Architecture Overview

📁 Backend Service

FastAPI application serving REST endpoints with Rasterio GIS processing, NumPy array operations, and SQLite route caching.

🧠 A* Graph Engine

Heapq-based 8-neighbor graph search calculating Minetti metabolic cost, Tobler speed decay, and hazard barriers.

🤖 AI Flight Director

Google Gemini API integration producing synthesized flight director safety briefings with client-side API key isolation.

🎨 Modern Frontend

Precompiled Tailwind v4 CLI, Vanilla ES modules, Leaflet map engine, and WCAG AA contrast accessibility.
